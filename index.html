ABOUT EDU BLOG

Education resources made easier to find.

EDU BLOG is a planned online learning hub for students, teachers and lifelong learners. The goal is to make useful educational content accessible, affordable and easy to use on phones and computers.

How the site may earn

  • Sell original notes and teaching resources.
  • Offer selected premium learning packs.
  • Consider advertising after the site has enough useful content and meets ad network requirements.

Only upload material you created or have permission to distribute. Do not sell copyrighted books, examination papers or other people's work without authorization.
